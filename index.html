Investment Philosophy

How We Think About Investing

Our philosophy is rooted in fundamental analysis, revenue growth, and long-term compounding. We don't chase momentum, trade on headlines, or time the market. We identify exceptional businesses and own them through full market cycles.

01
Revenue-Growth Oriented
We target companies with accelerating revenue growth, expanding margins, and clear trajectories toward market dominance — the compounding dynamics that drive substantial long-term appreciation.
02
Fundamental Analysis
Every position is researched bottom-up: business models, competitive moats, management quality, unit economics, and total addressable market. We invest in businesses, not tickers.
03
Concentrated Conviction
Our strategies hold 20–30 positions — not 500. When we have high conviction in a company's trajectory, we size the position to make it matter in the portfolio.
04
Innovation Focus
We invest in sectors driving the next decade of economic growth — AI, cloud, semiconductors, cybersecurity, fintech, and enterprise software. These are structural transformations, not trends.
05
Tax-Aware Execution
Every trade considers the tax consequence. We harvest losses systematically, optimize asset location, and coordinate with your CPA before executing tax-sensitive transactions.
06
Long-Term Compounding
Our average holding period is measured in years, not weeks. We let winners compound and exit positions only when the thesis breaks or the risk-reward changes materially.

The SMA Advantage

Why Direct Ownership Changes Everything

A Separately Managed Account means you own the individual stocks — not shares of a fund that owns stocks. This distinction unlocks tax-loss harvesting, asset location optimization, and complete transparency that pooled vehicles simply can't deliver.

Tax-Loss Harvesting

When individual positions decline, we sell at a loss to offset realized gains elsewhere in your portfolio — reducing your tax bill while maintaining market exposure through replacement securities.

  • ◆ Individual lot-level control — impossible inside fund wrappers
  • ◆ Losses offset gains across all accounts, including capital events
  • ◆ Excess losses carry forward indefinitely to offset future income
  • ◆ Coordinated with Brian Wendroff, CPA for optimal timing
